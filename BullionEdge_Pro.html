BullionEdge Pro · not financial advice. This is a planning, risk-management and journaling tool, built to improve the decisions you make before you click. It is not a signal service, it does not predict prices, and it cannot guarantee a profit. The buy/sell zone and the trade score are rules-based logic applied to levels you enter or load — the app has no view on where price is going. About the live price: gold spot comes from a free public feed and the candles come from the PAXG/USD market, a gold-backed proxy that tracks XAU/USD closely but not exactly. It is indicative data for reading structure — it is not your broker's dealing price, and you must confirm every level on your own platform before trading. Gold and leveraged FX are highly volatile; you can lose money, including on news gaps that jump straight past your stop. Before risking real capital: backtest the rules, forward-test on demo for weeks, and confirm your broker's contract size, pip size, spreads and swap rates — the defaults here assume a 1.00 price move is worth $100 per standard lot of gold, which not every broker matches. News times are recurring schedules, not dated events; always verify on a live calendar. Member profiles are local to this device and are not secure accounts.
