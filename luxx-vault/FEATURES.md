# Luxx Vault — portals and original features

Luxx Vault covers everything the reference site does (see PARITY.md) and goes further in the areas where Luxx4less has an advantage: a real shop in Antipolo, testing equipment, live selling, resellers, and an audience built since 2019.

The site has **four portals**, one per type of user. Each feature is tagged with the phase it's built in (see PROMPT.md §16) and **★** for a Luxx Vault original that the reference doesn't have. Items marked `[CONFIRM]` need a legal or business check before launch.

---

## 1. Owner Studio — for Luxx4less (you)

Where you add and manage **your own** gold, diamonds and gemstones. Your products always show first as the official store.

### Add a product in under a minute
- **From your phone:** take photos → the background is cleaned and a watermark added automatically → pick a category → enter karat and weight → done. (4, ★)
- **Choose how it's priced:**
  - **Live per-gram price:** you set ₱/g on top of spot, or a fixed markup, and the price moves with the market. You can set a floor and a ceiling so it never goes too low or high. This matches how you already price on lives (e.g. ₱7,700–7,800/g). (4, ★)
  - **Fixed price:** for diamonds, gemstones and designer pieces. (4)
- **Details:** gold type (Saudi, Japan, Italian, HK), karat, exact grams, stone details (4Cs, certificate), ring size or chain length, pawnable yes/no, receipt or certificate included. (4)
- **One-of-one items:** most jewellery is a single piece with its own weight, so each item gets its own code and sells out once sold. Repeat items such as bars and coins get stock counts. (4)
- **Bulk upload** from a spreadsheet, **duplicate** a product, save **drafts**, **schedule** a launch. (4)
- **Print QR tags:** each piece gets a small printed tag with its code. Scanning it opens the piece's authenticity page on our site. (4, ★)

### Selling tools
- **Live-selling board:** during a Facebook live you show item codes, and viewers tap "Mine!" on the site instead of commenting. The first claim reserves the item with a payment timer, and an invoice is created automatically. That replaces the "mine" comment chaos and missed buyers. (7, ★)
- **Hulugan (layaway) plans:** you set the down payment %, the number of months, and a grace period. Reminders go out automatically, and the item is reserved until fully paid. (4)
- **Buy / sell spreads per karat:** one screen sets "we buy at" and "we sell at" across the site. (3)
- **Flash sales and "gold dip" promos** that start when spot drops below a level you set. (8, ★)
- **Daily price card for your socials:** an image with today's per-gram prices in Luxx4less branding, ready to post on FB and IG. (3, ★)
- **Orders, payments and shipping:** order board, bank-transfer proof checking, and courier tracking numbers. (4)
- **Sales dashboard:** best sellers, sales by karat and category, layaway balances, live-selling results. (8)

### Buying gold from customers
- **"Sell to us" queue:** customer quotes from the website land here. You confirm or adjust the offer and book an appraisal. (7)
- **Trade-in credit:** a customer's old gold becomes store credit toward a new piece. (7, ★)

## 2. Seller portal — verified third-party sellers

For other people selling gold and gems on the marketplace. Only verified sellers (Tier 4: ID, liveness, proof of address) can list.

- **Listing wizard:** photos → metal, karat and weight → the **melt value and a suggested price range** appear live → set a price or "open for offers". (6)
- **Premium vs spot shown before publishing:** warns if the price is far below melt value, a scam signal. (6)
- **Spot-pegged listings:** "spot + 8%" re-prices itself as the market moves. (6, ★)
- **Luxx-Tested badge:** the seller brings the item to Luxx4less Antipolo for XRF or acid testing. The listing gets a "Luxx-Tested" badge with the result and date. Buyers trust it more, and you earn a testing fee. (6, ★) `[CONFIRM fee]`
- **Consignment:** the seller leaves the item with Luxx4less. You hold it and ship it, and the seller is paid after the sale, the safest trade for both sides. (6, ★) `[CONFIRM terms]`
- **Instant offer vs listing:** before listing, the seller sees "Luxx4less can buy this now for ₱X". A quick sale for them, inventory for you. (6, ★)
- **Buyer requests matched automatically:** when a buyer posts "looking for 18K Saudi chain, 10–15 g", matching sellers are notified. (6)
- **Offers inbox, chat, orders and payouts** in one place. (6)
- **Seller stats:** views, saves and offers per listing; trust score; seller levels (New → Trusted → Top Seller). (6)
- **Limits for new sellers:** a cap on listing count and value until their first completed trades. (6)

## 3. Buyer portal — customers

- **"My Vault":** record the gold you own, bought here or anywhere, with karat and weight, and see its **live value** today. Receipts and certificates are stored with each piece. It fits the name, and it brings people back daily. (6, ★)
- **Shop by budget:** "I have ₱15,000" shows what fits, and how many grams of each karat that buys today. (4, ★)
- **Price lock at checkout:** the price is held for 15 minutes so it doesn't change mid-payment. (4, ★)
- **Budget alerts:** "tell me when this necklace drops under ₱20,000" or "when 18K goes below ₱X/g". (3)
- **Buyback promise:** pieces bought from Luxx4less carry a QR certificate with a published buyback rate. (4, ★) `[CONFIRM terms]`
- **Pawn value estimate:** a rough guide to what a pawnshop may lend on a piece, clearly labelled as an estimate. (3, ★)
- **Buyer requests:** post "wanted: 21K bangle, 20 g" for sellers to respond to. (6)
- **Offers, chat, protected payment, dispute button, reviews.** (6)
- **Wishlist, compare up to 3, recently viewed.** (4)
- **Gift mode:** gift wrap, message card, hide the price on the receipt, ship to the recipient. (4, ★)
- **Ring size finder:** printable sizer plus the "measure a ring you own" method. (3)
- **Book an appointment:** in-person viewing, testing or appraisal at the Antipolo shop. (7)

## Trust tools anyone can use (★)

- **"Is this really Luxx4less?":** paste a phone number, GCash name or Facebook page link to check whether it's an official Luxx4less channel. It stops scammers who pretend to be you. (7)
- **Hallmark decoder:** what 916, 750, 875, 585 and common Saudi, Japan and Italian stamps mean. (7)
- **Price-check any offer:** paste a price and weight to see whether it's fair, or suspiciously cheap, against today's melt value. (3)
- **Scam report:** report a user, listing or outside number. (6) A public list of reported numbers needs a Data Privacy Act review first. `[CONFIRM]`
